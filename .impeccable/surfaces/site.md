## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an atlas plate of the argument. Regional Security Complex Theory is drawn as a map of complexes, so the site speaks in the language of strategic atlases: flat regional fields, boundaries, map lettering and a graticule. It refuses the academic-portfolio default of cream paper, italic display serif and tracked mono labels.

OWN-WORLD: Pale sea-blue ground with a faint graticule; land-white plates with 1px map-rule borders and small square corners; three regional field tints (Central Asia ochre, South Asia sage, Middle East and Gulf rose) used as whole fields, never sprinkles; lapis (Afghanistan's own stone) as the single ink for actions, links and Afghanistan itself; navy text. Archivo in expanded width for map-style headings and labels, Archivo normal for interface, Source Serif 4 for long reading. Night chart for dark: deep navy sea, same roles.

STORY: A supervisor sees the argument as a map, understands the claim in one sentence, sees three projects leading to the doctorate, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the claim set as a map cartouche (H1 in expanded Archivo, second line in lapis), the intro, the status line and two actions (See the PhD proposal; Download CV). Right, the portrait plate. Directly below, full width, Fig. 1: the schematic map of the three complexes with Afghanistan in lapis and the Insulator/Instigator switch; stat plates sit beside it as a map legend.

FORM: Regional atlas, position 1 of 7 on the hand-derived list. seed: none (unavailable). The launcher that runs concept-seed was blocked in this session; the direction was chosen by the user from four presented cards.

SIGNATURE: switching the reading redraws the borders: Insulator draws hatched boundary walls around Afghanistan; Instigator lifts them and lapis flows travel into each complex. One authored motion moment; everything else is still.
