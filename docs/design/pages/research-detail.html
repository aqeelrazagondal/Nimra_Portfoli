I examine Afghanistan as a driver of regional security dynamics, rather than a buffer between neighbouring security complexes.

03 · THE ARGUMENT

Regional Security Complex Theory classifies Afghanistan as an “insulator”: a state that sits between complexes and keeps their dynamics apart. My research challenges that classification and explores Afghanistan’s role as an “instigator” of regional security dynamics.
